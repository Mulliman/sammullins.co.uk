## Why

Sam (14 November 2026): keep the feel (simple, clean, spacious, premium, monochrome apart from what needs attention) but bring every component up to a professional 2026 standard. Simple cards filled the screen, the site was static, and the home page still led with photography. Earlier home experiments that leaned on a brighter orange and big imagery were rejected.

The brand distillation, before and after screenshots, motion recordings, peer reviews and the judge's verdict are in `design-review-2026-11/` beside the repo, and on a private review page.

## Scope

- Tokens: `--radius-m`, `--radius-pill`, `--step-7`, `--leading-hero`, motion tokens (`--ease-out`, `--dur-reveal`, `--rise`); theme tokens `--color-border-strong`, `--shadow-card`, `--color-inverse-border-strong`, `--color-hero-mark(-soft)`.
- Motion: one orchestrated moment, the hero entrance with the bar mark building in. Everything else answers an action: hover responses (card edge, call-to-action underline, nav hairline, row wash) on hover-capable devices only, press feedback; a frosted stuck header; animated disclosures and mobile menu; cross-document View Transitions always on (ticket 905 prototype A shipped, flag removed), render-blocked on `#content` so a page never fades in half drawn. A first pass also faded content in on scroll, lifted cards with a shadow on hover and put an arrow on every button; all three were cut on 14 November 2026 as template tells (Sam installed the frontend-design skill, which names them).
- Components: cards (16:9, hairline, left-aligned, text call to action, phone rows, lone-card feature), logo wall (hairline grid 3/4/6), hero (`ctas`, `mark`; no thick frame), buttons (solid and ghost, no arrow), listing, timeline (title first, meta line beneath), callout, quote, steps, next/prev, disclosure, code radius, header, footer (left-aligned grid), agent view toggle, 404 links.
- Content: home without photography (plain hero with actions, no kingfishers, no CaptureOne copy, no footer Photography link; the PanoCapture and PostCapture cards stay, as software); software regrouped into one band per audience; CV sentence case and an About/Contact band.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] No colour literals outside `src/styles/themes/`; no inline styles or scripts.
- [x] With reduced motion, or without JS, nothing is hidden and nothing animates.
- [x] No photography on the home page (no photo hero, no kingfishers). The CaptureOne plugins stay as software (Sam, 26 November 2026).
- [x] `pnpm verify` passes.
- [x] Sam picks a direction: A, "Refined" (this branch). B, "Paper" (a light hero, hairline bands, an editorial split and greyscale images), was dropped on 14 November 2026.
- [x] Sam confirms the View Transitions, footer link and home card decisions (26 November 2026: View Transitions on everywhere; the footer Photography link goes; PanoCapture and PostCapture stay on the home page, with The Imposter as a feature card above a row of four).
