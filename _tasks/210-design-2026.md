## Why

Sam (14 November 2026): keep the feel (simple, clean, spacious, premium, monochrome apart from what needs attention) but bring every component up to a professional 2026 standard. Simple cards filled the screen, the site was static, and the home page still led with photography. Earlier home experiments that leaned on a brighter orange and big imagery were rejected.

The brand distillation, before and after screenshots, motion recordings, peer reviews and the judge's verdict are in `design-review-2026-11/` beside the repo, and on a private review page.

## Scope

- Tokens: `--radius-m`, `--radius-pill`, `--step-7`, `--leading-hero`, motion tokens (`--ease-out`, `--dur-reveal`, `--rise`, `--stagger`); theme tokens `--color-border-strong`, `--shadow-card`, `--color-inverse-border-strong`, `--color-hero-mark(-soft)`.
- Motion: reveal on scroll (`Reveal.astro`, one-shot, below the fold only, off with reduced motion or no JS); the hero entrance and the bar mark building in; hover responses (card lift, arrow nudge, nav hairline, row wash) on hover-capable devices only; a frosted stuck header; animated disclosures and mobile menu; cross-document View Transitions always on (ticket 905 prototype A shipped, flag removed).
- Components: cards (16:9, hairline, left-aligned, arrow link, phone rows, lone-card feature), logo wall (hairline grid 3/4/6), hero (`ctas`, `mark`; no thick frame), buttons (solid and ghost), listing, timeline (title first, meta line beneath), callout, quote, steps, next/prev, disclosure, code radius, header, footer (left-aligned grid), agent view toggle, 404 links.
- Content: home without photography (plain hero with actions, no kingfishers, no CaptureOne cards or copy, no footer Photography link); software regrouped into one band per audience; CV sentence case and an About/Contact band.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] No colour literals outside `src/styles/themes/`; no inline styles or scripts.
- [x] With reduced motion, or without JS, nothing is hidden and nothing animates.
- [x] Nothing photo-related on the home page.
- [x] `pnpm verify` passes.
- [x] Sam picks a direction: A, "Refined" (this branch). B, "Paper" (a light hero, hairline bands, an editorial split and greyscale images), was dropped on 14 November 2026.
- [ ] Sam confirms the View Transitions, footer link and home card decisions (listed on the review page).
