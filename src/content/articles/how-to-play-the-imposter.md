The Imposter is an online party game I made for spies and sleuths. This short animated video explains how to play the beta.

## What you need

A group of friends and a smartphone (or similar device) each. You don't have to be in the same room; it works over a video call, or even over the phone. You can install the app for the best experience, or just play in a web browser.

## Setting up

Every game needs a host, who starts and manages the game. Everyone else joins with the five digit code the host shares. The host then picks a grid of words from a category, such as animals, celebrities or chocolate bars.

## A round

1. One player is secretly chosen as the imposter.
2. Everyone can see the secret word on the grid, except the imposter.
3. Each player says a single word that proves they know the secret word, in any order. In the video the word is "white". The other players say "wedding" and "house", while the imposter, who doesn't know the word, guesses from "greenhouse" and "green card" and plays safe with "tree".
4. Players can then interrogate each other. Just don't give the answer away.
5. Everyone accuses who they think the imposter was, and wagers points on how confident they are.
6. The imposter is revealed, but they get one last chance to guess the secret word.

## Scoring

Points are awarded for both team and individual success. The more you wager, the more you win, but if you get it wrong the imposter may take your points.

Each round should take less than ten minutes, and you can play as many as you like.
