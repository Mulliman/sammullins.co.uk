Once your processes exist, applying them in Capture One takes a couple of clicks.

1. Right-click a photo, go to **Edit with** and choose the PostCapture process option.
2. PostCapture checks the photo's metadata against your processes, in priority order, and applies the first match. Capture One shows a notification when it's done, and the edited copy appears next to the original.

In the video, the first photo had no colour label, so it got the default red border. I then gave the photo a yellow colour label in its metadata and ran it again, and this time it got the yellow border from the yellow process.

Next, [use PostCapture in process recipes](/articles/postcapture-in-process-recipes/) to do the same on export.

Download PostCapture from the [GitHub page](https://mulliman.github.io/PostCapture/).
