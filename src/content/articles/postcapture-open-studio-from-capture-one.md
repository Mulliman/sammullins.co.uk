You don't have to leave Capture One to make a PostCapture process. Right-click a photo, go to **Edit with** and choose the PostCapture Studio option.

Studio opens with that photo already set as the example image, so **Populate rule from example image** can read its metadata straight away. In the video I picked a field from the list, named the process and created it, ready to add operations as in [create an advanced process](/articles/postcapture-create-an-advanced-process/).

Next, [run a process on an image](/articles/postcapture-run-a-process-on-an-image/).

Download PostCapture from the [GitHub page](https://mulliman.github.io/PostCapture/).
