      - type: "prose"
        markdown: |-
          Each round one player is the chooser. They pick a topic, such as British Biscuits, and a question to go with it, such as "by personal preference". They then pick five items from the topic.

          The chooser secretly ranks the five items from S (best) to D (worst), with one item in each tier.

          Everyone else ranks the same five items the way they think the chooser did. Nobody sees anyone else's ranking until everyone has submitted.

          Then the chooser's ranking is revealed one tier at a time, from D up to S, with each guesser's accuracy shown beside it.

          The closer your ranking is to the chooser's, the more points you score. A ranking that matches exactly scores 100 points for the round. The chooser scores half the average of the guessers' points, so the chooser is not left out.

          A match is five rounds, ten rounds or endless, as the host chooses. At the end there is a podium, and awards such as Mind Reader for the most exact tiers, and Steady Hand for the lowest average distance.
