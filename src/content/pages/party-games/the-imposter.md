      - type: "prose"
        markdown: |-
          The host creates a game, and everyone else joins with the five-digit code. The game needs at least three players before a round can start.

          Each round, everyone sees the same grid of 16 words, from a topic such as actors. Everyone except the imposter is told which one is the secret word. The imposter is chosen at random, and isn't told.

          Each player then gives a clue about the secret word: one word or a short phrase. Clues are public as soon as they're given.

          If talk it over is on, everyone discusses the clues before anyone accuses. The host decides when to move on, and the timer on screen doesn't end the round by itself.

          Everyone accuses one other player. The non-imposters also bet 1, 2 or 3 points on being right. The imposter accuses someone too, as a scapegoat, but without a bet.

          The most accused player is the suspect. If the group catches the imposter, everyone else scores a point, and each player who accused the imposter wins their bet. If the imposter isn't caught, they score a point. Guessing the secret word from the 16 options earns a bonus point, and takes the bets of anyone who accused the wrong player.

          A match is five rounds, ten rounds or endless, as the host chooses. At the end there are awards, such as Best Liar for the most rounds escaped as the imposter, and Bloodhound for the most correct accusations.
