      - type: "prose"
        markdown: |-
          Everyone sees the same prompt: an obscure word, a bizarre acronym, a weird historical law or a forgotten film title. The host picks the kind of prompt, or a mix of all four.

          Each player secretly writes a convincing fake answer, a bluff. The real answer is in the mix too, and nobody knows which answer is the real one.

          All the answers are shuffled together. Someone is picked to read them out loud, and everyone votes for the one they believe is genuine. You can't vote for your own bluff.

          Scoring rewards both sides. You get a point for each player who votes for your bluff, and two points for voting for the truth. If your bluff gets more votes than every other answer, including the truth, you get a bonus point.

          Then the reveal happens one card at a time. The host paces it, and everyone sees who wrote each bluff and who fell for it.

          A match is five rounds, ten rounds or endless, as the host chooses. At the end there are awards, such as Biggest Blowhard for the most players fooled, and Lie Detector for the most truths spotted.
