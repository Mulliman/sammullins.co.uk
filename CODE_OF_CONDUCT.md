# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be kind, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work rather than the person. Harassment of any kind isn't welcome here.

It applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and anything else in this repository.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, contact me privately through one of the profiles linked in the footer of [www.sammullins.co.uk](https://www.sammullins.co.uk/). I'm the only maintainer, so reports come straight to me, and I'll keep them confidential.

I may edit or remove comments, issues or pull requests that break this code, and block people who keep doing so.
